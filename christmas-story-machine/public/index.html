How it works

  1. Put the invite QR code on the TV or fridge. Guests scan it — no app, no accounts.
  2. They tap Tell a story, Share a drawing or Share a photo. Stories become illustrated, narrated scenes; drawings and photos are kept exactly as they are.
  3. Everything is prepared in the background as it arrives. You can read, tweak or hold anything before the reveal.
  4. Press Compile, then Start the reveal on the big screen. Everyone gets a private link to watch and download it afterwards.
Made with love for families. Everything stays private to your host and your family.
